=== CORRECTION (2026-11-23): "a short tap does not open an app" WAS AN adb ARTIFACT, MY EARLIER READING WAS WRONG ===
What I wrote above in the section "OPEN AN APP FROM THE LENS GRID" ("This is the design, not a fault", "a short tap on an icon launches nothing", "I did not find a user-facing line that tells a new user this") is NOT supported. I tested only with `adb shell input tap`, which sends DOWN and UP about 1 ms apart, and then built a design explanation out of the code. A later measurement on the same release build (apk 8c4f48075314, HEAD 05c92e9a, KJ7, gate first, no ad window, restore DATA-IDENTICAL 207 files):
- `input tap` on the Calculator icon: stayed on ActHome in 7 of 7 rounds.
- Finger held in place with `input swipe x y x y <ms>`: 1 ms and 5 ms stayed (4 of 4 each); 10, 16 and 33 ms opened Calculator (4 of 4 each); 20, 60, 100, 150, 200 and 300 ms opened it (3 of 3 each). Earlier single trials: 400 ms and 900 ms gave the app menu, which follows the system long-press time (300 ms on this phone, `long_press_timeout=300`).
So the threshold sits between 5 and 10 ms. The probable cause, from a comment in LensView (the selected icon index is only refreshed when the grid is drawn, one frame is 16.7 ms at 60 Hz): a touch released before the next frame has no selected icon and launches nothing. This is read from code, not proven with a debugger.
A finger tap on a phone usually lasts tens of milliseconds, so it should open the app, but I did not test with a finger, so I cannot say so for certain.
Consequences: (1) my chat message that new users and Play reviewers tapping the grid "would see nothing happen" had no support; (2) the "first-run hint to lift the finger" I offered was built on that wrong premise and is not needed on this evidence; (3) the release-notes section was rewritten. Not a defect of the app that I can show: a sub-10 ms touch is not something a hand produces.
