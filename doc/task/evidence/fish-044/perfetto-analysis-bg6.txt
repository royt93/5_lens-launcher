Key finding:
The +2.3 ms gap in SurfaceFlinger FrameTimeline is NOT CPU/GPU bottleneck (UI+RenderThread delta is only +0.29 ms).
Instead, 99.8% of frames with all effects on are flagged as "Buffer Stuffing" by SurfaceFlinger.
This occurs because postInvalidateOnAnimation() (from inertia / snap) and ACTION_MOVE invalidate() submit buffers faster than SurfaceFlinger's latch cadence, causing buffers to queue up.
True jank (App Deadline Missed) is only 1-2 frames out of 550 (< 0.4%), confirming the app does not drop frames.
