Idea "xem trước lens kế bên khi ngón vào dải cạnh" (FISH-049, 2026-11-14) — probe with the real constants. NOT built.
Sources: EdgeSwipePolicy (HORIZONTAL_DOMINANCE_RATIO 1.5, TOUCH_SLOP_MULTIPLIER 2.0), LensView (edgeThresholdPx = 32 dp), device size and density read from adb.
ViewConfiguration touch slop assumed 8 dp (the usual Android value; not read from the device).

Device       edge band            trigger distance (|dx| > 2 * touch slop)      label of 8 characters (14 sp)
A50s         84 px  (7.8% width)   42 px (16 dp, 3.9% of width)                  ~162 px at font 1.0, ~210 px at font 1.3
KJ7          96 px  (8.9% width)   48 px (16 dp, 4.4% of width)                  ~185 px at font 1.0, ~240 px at font 1.3
Pixel 7 Pro  112 px (7.8% width)   56 px (16 dp, 3.9% of width)                  ~216 px at font 1.0, ~280 px at font 1.3

Time to cover the 16 dp trigger distance: about 32 ms at 500 dp/s and 16 ms at 1000 dp/s (one or two frames at 60 Hz).
Reading: the swipe from the edge band commits (and switches the lens) after only 16 dp of horizontal travel, so a label that appears when the finger enters the band
would be on screen for one or two frames before the lens changes and Home already shows the new lens name in tvLensName. A label wider than the whole band (162-280 px vs
84-112 px) would also not fit inside it. To make the preview readable the gesture would need a new hold threshold (about 200-250 ms), which is a new gesture to discover and
competes with long press, with the same "right but not visible in practice" risk that cancelled FISH-045.
